## What it does

A complete ISP billing and management system in PHP and MySQL, built for providers who bill monthly, collect in the field and need the books to balance. It covers the whole chain — lead, customer, connection, package, invoice, payment, ledger — plus the counter POS for routers and cables and the collection round your staff run door to door. What it does Customers are registered with everything an ISP actually needs: CNIC, GPS location, area, package, installation and due date, IP and MAC address, connection type (PPPoE, static or DHCP) and medium (fibre, wireless, ethernet or DSL). Once a month you generate every bill in one pass, with a preview-and-confirm step before anything is written.
