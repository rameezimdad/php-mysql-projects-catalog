## What it does

Recurring revenue is a billing problem before it is a sales problem. This is the system that runs it: subscriptions that renew themselves, invoices raised on schedule, reminders that go out before a renewal instead of after it lapses, and a portal where the customer handles their own account. The billing engine Subscriptions carry their own cycle and renew automatically, with the recurring billing engine generating the invoice and tracking what has been paid against it. A cron-driven email reminder goes out ahead of each upcoming renewal — the single change that recovers the most silently lapsed subscriptions. Amounts are multi-currency, so customers in different markets are billed in theirs. Seeing the book The same subscriptions read as a Kanban board or a full calendar, depending on whether you are thinking in stages or in dates.
