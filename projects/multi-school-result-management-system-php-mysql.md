## What it does

A multi-school result management platform in PHP and MySQL, built as a SaaS rather than a single-school install. One deployment serves many schools, each with its own branches, staff, students and data, and each on its own subscription plan. What it does A school signs up, picks a plan and pays by Stripe or bank transfer. The plan sets the limits — students, teachers and branches — and carries an expiry, so Trial, Starter, Standard and Premium tiers behave differently without separate code. Inside a school, marks are entered through the UI or imported in bulk from Excel, results pass an approval step before they publish, and report cards print from more than ten templates that can be assigned per class. Parents look up a published result from a public search page without logging in.
