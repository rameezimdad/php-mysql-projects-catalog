## What it does

A form builder that goes past the usual text-and-dropdown set: twenty-two field types, appointment slots, QR scanning and cascading dropdowns, with the AI drafting the form from a description of what you need. Twenty-two field types, including the awkward ones Dependent cascading dropdowns — pick a country and the city list narrows — are a genuine pain to hand-build and are here as a field type. So is appointment booking with slot selection, which turns a form into a booking system, and a QR code scanner for submissions where the visitor scans a code rather than typing a reference. Controls on the form itself A form can carry an expiry date and a submission limit, so a registration closes on its own when the date passes or the places run out — without you remembering to switch it off.
