## What it does

A pharmacy management system in PHP and MySQL built around the problem a general POS cannot solve: medicine has batches and expiry dates. Stock is tracked per batch, expiry is visible before it becomes dead stock, and billing pulls from the right batch instead of a single flat quantity. What it does The counter runs on a POS billing screen with barcode scanning, so a sale is scanned, priced and printed without typing product names. Behind it, purchases are recorded against suppliers and feed medicine inventory batch by batch. Accounting reports, sales and purchase reports and Chart.js dashboards show where the money went, HR payroll handles staff salaries, and a ChatGPT-powered assistant is built into the panel.
