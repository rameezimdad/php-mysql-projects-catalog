## What it does

What it does This gym management system in PHP and MySQL runs the front desk of one gym or fitness club. Staff register members with a photo, sell and renew membership plans, take full or part payments and print thermal receipts, A4 invoices and QR member cards. The Check-In desk admits a member by member number, phone or QR code, checks the plan and its grace days, and keeps the attendance record. It also covers personal training sessions with trainer commission, group classes with a waitlist and a printable roster, body measurements, follow-ups and enquiries, expenses, day close and reports for revenue, profit and loss, attendance and membership status. A gym POS sells products at the counter, and the public website shows your plans, blog and an online shop whose orders land in the admin panel.
