## What it does

Complete installment software built in PHP and MySQL for shops that sell mobile phones, motorbikes and home appliances on monthly payments. The customer pays an advance at the counter, then a fixed amount every month. The app keeps the customer, the sale, every payment, the balance and who is late in one place, and it works on a phone at the counter. Customers and sales Each customer carries a photo, ID card copies (front and back), mobile and address. A new sale starts with a tap on the item type (phone, bike, TV, washing machine and more), then the product. The price, advance and number of months fill in from your own rate card, and the payment schedule builds live: the advance today, then equal monthly amounts with the last month taking the rest. Add up to two guarantors, check the review and save.
