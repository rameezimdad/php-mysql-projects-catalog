## What it does

A complete multi-branch hotel management system built in PHP and MySQL — one dashboard that runs the front desk, housekeeping, the restaurant till, the event halls and the staff, across as many properties as you own. Switch branches from the top bar and the whole system reloads on that branch's data. Front desk and reservations Guests land on the public page, search rooms by arrival and departure date, and send a booking request. The request lands in the Booking Requests queue, where the receptionist confirms it into a reservation or replies on WhatsApp. At arrival, check in and assign a live room; move a guest to another room (a reason is required, so the move is logged); print the A4 registration pass; and check out through the folio with a manager write-off. The tape chart shows the whole week colour-coded — booked, in-house, checked out, out of order and unassigned.
