## What it does

What it does This digital product selling website in PHP and MySQL is a store you host yourself. You list source code, templates, plugins, ebooks and bundles, and customers buy them from a public storefront with a shop page, product pages, a cart and a checkout. After payment the customer downloads from My Purchases through a signed link that works for a few minutes, with a download limit per version, so a shared link is of no use to anyone else. Payments go through Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay or PayFast, or by bank transfer where the buyer uploads a receipt and your staff approve it. The admin side covers products with versions and private file storage, orders and refunds, customers, coupons and flash sales, reviews, support tickets, a help center and blog, newsletter announcements and sales reports.
