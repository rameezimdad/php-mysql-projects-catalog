## What it does

A complete GST billing and inventory system in PHP and MySQL. It handles the part most billing scripts get wrong: the tax itself. Intra-state sales split automatically into SGST and CGST, inter-state sales switch to IGST, HSN codes sit on the line items, and the invoice total prints in words the way a tax invoice is expected to. What it does Sales run from quotation to proforma to GST tax invoice, with barcode scanning at entry and barcode label printing for stock. Purchases run through supplier purchase orders that feed live stock, with reorder levels and low-stock alerts so you find out before an item runs out. Customers carry an outstanding balance and a printable statement, payments in and out post against the ledger, and sales returns calculate their own credit.
