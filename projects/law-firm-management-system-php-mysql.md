## What it does

Case management for a working law chamber: every client opening into their matters, payments, hearings and documents from one screen, a court calendar that stops a date being missed, and an AI assistant on top of the firm's own data. The client 360 and the case file Open a client and you get everything at once — their cases, what they have paid, their next hearing, their documents. Each case file holds the parties, running notes, and uploads that are not limited to PDFs: images and video attach to the matter as well, which is how evidence actually arrives. You can call or email the client straight from the case screen. Hearings, appointments and money The court calendar and hearing scheduler keep the diary in one place. Appointments are booked with their fee tracked against them.
