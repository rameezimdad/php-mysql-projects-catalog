## What it does

A complete NFC attendance system in PHP and MySQL. Staff tap an NFC sticker on a door with their Android phone, and the visit is saved with the server time and a comment as proof that they were really there. No biometric machine, Arduino or extra hardware is needed: just low-cost NFC stickers and the phones your team already carries. What it does Each sticker belongs to a location such as a washroom, corridor, entrance, office or store room. One tap records one visit. The first visit of the day checks the employee in and the last one becomes the check-out, so attendance fills itself in. The dashboard shows who is on duty right now, today's visits and open complaints, and every location opens into a 360-degree view of its visitors, stickers, complaints and timeline.
