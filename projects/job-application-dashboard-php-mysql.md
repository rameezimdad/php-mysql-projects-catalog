## What it does

An applicant tracking system with the piece most PHP job portals skip: a Kanban pipeline you drag candidates across, so the state of every hire is visible at a glance instead of buried in a status column. The pipeline Candidates move between stages by drag and drop, and the stages themselves are yours to define — screening, telephonic, technical, HR, offer, whatever your process is called. The board is the working screen; the list view behind it filters and exports to CSV or PDF when someone wants the data outside the app. The candidate record Full create, read, update and delete on candidates, with resumes and certificates uploaded as PDF, DOC or DOCX. Academic and technical qualifications are multi-select, so a candidate can carry several without a row per qualification.
