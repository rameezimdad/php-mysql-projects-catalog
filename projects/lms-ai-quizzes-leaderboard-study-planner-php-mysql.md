## What it does

A learning platform built around one idea: the quiz should come from the student's own notes. Upload a photograph of a handwritten page, Gemini reads it, and the questions that follow are about what that student actually has to learn — not a generic question bank. Notes in, questions out A student photographs their notes and the AI extracts the text, which can be played back as audio for revision on the move. Notes are saved to a history, filed into folders by subject, and tagged — difficult, easy, exam preparation — so a term's material stays findable. From any note, the system generates a quiz at the difficulty level chosen, and explains why each answer was right rather than only marking it. Pre-quiz and post-quiz — the measurement This is the part worth understanding.
