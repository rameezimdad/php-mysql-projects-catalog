## What it does

A complete vehicle insurance management system in PHP and MySQL. One dashboard runs the whole agency — policies, customers, vehicles, agents, claims, accounts and reports — so renewals stop slipping through the cracks and agent commissions match the bank at month end. What it does The dashboard opens on premium at risk, this period's numbers and every policy that needs attention, each with a one-click WhatsApp renewal reminder. A policy records the vehicle, customer, branch, insurer, premium split and how it was paid, and opens into a 360-degree view with commissions, payments, documents, endorsements and claims. Hundreds of policies load from an Excel template in one go; a bad row is rejected with the reason, so wrong data never reaches your records. Deleted policies go to a Trash you can restore from.
