## What it does

A complete cashbook built in PHP and MySQL — one dashboard that replaces the register you keep your daily cash in and cash out in. Every entry you post updates the book, the party ledgers, the receipts and the reports together. Every entry, with its proof and its history Cash in and cash out entries carry amount, date, party, category, payment mode, remark, cheque or invoice reference and a receipt image. Edit one and the system marks it edited and keeps the full before-and-after in an edit history. Each entry prints its own PDF voucher, and a running balance column walks from your opening balance down the book. The dashboard has a Quick Entry panel for the entries you make in a hurry. Parties, ledgers and statements Keep clients, suppliers and employees in one master with contact number, email, tax number, address and opening balance.
